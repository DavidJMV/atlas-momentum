## 🔍 Detailed Pros & Cons

### FTMO Advantages ✅
1. **Higher Success Rate**: 37.5% vs 20.8%
2. **Two-Phase System**: Verification phase acts as safety net (90% pass rate)
3. **Higher Drawdown Tolerance**: 10% vs 5%
4. **Fewer Trading Days Required**: 4 vs 7 days
5. **Proven Track Record**: Established reputation

### FTMO Disadvantages ❌
1. **Aggressive Profit Target**: 10% monthly target very challenging
2. **Daily Loss Limit**: 5% daily limit can trigger during normal drawdowns
3. **CFD Instruments**: Spreads and overnight costs impact futures strategies
4. **Higher Cost**: £400 per attempt vs $150
5. **Currency Risk**: GBP-based when strategy optimized for USD futures

### Apex Trader Funding Advantages ✅
1. **Reasonable Profit Target**: 6% monthly target more achievable
2. **No Daily Loss Limit**: Major advantage for drawdown recovery
3. **Futures-Native**: Direct futures trading, no CFD conversions
4. **Lower Cost**: $150 per attempt vs £400
5. **USD-Based**: Matches strategy currency optimization
6. **Single-Phase**: Pass once and get funded

### Apex Trader Funding Disadvantages ❌
1. **Lower Success Rate**: 20.8% vs 37.5%
2. **Tighter Drawdown**: 5% max drawdown vs 10%
3. **More Trading Days**: 7 days minimum vs 4
4. **Single Chance**: No verification phase safety net
5. **Newer Platform**: Less established than FTMO
